# Kobold Campout

For 2–4 players, a slice of life TTRPG using one deck of cards. An improv conversation through the eyes of kids in the realm of Orrelia.

Kobold Campout is meant to evoke the likes of a comic strip; Calvin and Hobbes walking through the woods talking to each other, dictated and led by your hand of playing cards.

> Within the Brightmist Wood, a young kobold and their friends slip away from camp while their parents set up tents and cook dinner. What follows is a quiet afternoon of wandering, pretending, and discovery; unremarkable events to grown-ups, but grand and enchanting to youngsters.
>
> You are those youngsters, and this is how you spend the afternoon.

---

## You Need

- 1 standard playing card deck (jokers removed)
- 1 character card per player
- Pencils
- The rules

---

## Characters

You play as a young elf, dwarf, goblin, and kobold walking through the forest northeast of Glimmerhold, on a camping trip with the kobold's family. The parents are back at the campsite setting up and making dinner.

At the beginning of the game, remove the aces from the deck, shuffle them, and deal one to each player.

- **Heart** = elf
- **Diamond** = dwarf
- **Spade** = goblin
- **Club** = kobold

Choose a name for your kid and describe how they look.

Draw a card for your kid's personality:

| Card | Personality |
| --- | ---|
| Heart | Honest |
| Diamond | Brave |
| Spade | Lazy |
| Club | Grumpy |
| Jack | Cowardly |
| Queen | Sassy |
| King | Generous |

### Questions

Before play begins, each player asks one question to the player on their left and one to the player on their right. Write down the answers on your character sheet.

> Treat answers as true for the rest of the game, even if they change how you imagine your character later.

**Elf**
- Ask left: *What did you see that you weren't supposed to?*
- Ask right: *What do you think is the prettiest thing out here?*

**Dwarf**
- Ask left: *What did you bring that nobody else thought to bring?*
- Ask right: *What are you going to tell everyone when we get back?*

**Goblin**
- Ask left: *What's the best thing that's happened so far?*
- Ask right: *What should we definitely do even if it's a bad idea?*

**Kobold**
- Ask left: *What did you find that you wish you could keep forever?*
- Ask right: *What makes you feel safe when the woods get too quiet?*

Then write your **Truth** on your character card. This is one sentence about who your character is at their core, think about your kid's personlity and who they are.

Once questions are answered and your Truth is written, shuffle all cards back into the deck.

---

## To Start

Deal **3 cards** to each player.

Place the top 3 cards of the deck in the centre of the table. These are the locations where your adventure takes place. Reveal the first one, this is the first location you're walking through.

### Locations

The three locations form the shape of your afternoon:

1. **Somewhere you find** - a discovery, something that catches your eye
2. **Somewhere something happens** - the heart of the adventure
3. **Somewhere you head back from** - the walk back toward camp and dinner

> These are not geographic labels, but emotional phases of the afternoon.

### Reading a Location Card

| Colour | Weather  |
| --- | --- |
| Red   | Sunny, breezy, calm |
| Black   | Cloudy, rain, cold|

| Suit | Location |
| --- | ---|
| Heart | Meadow |
| Diamond | Rocky Outcrop |
| Spade | Creek |
| Club | Thicket |

| Value | Vibe |
| --- | ---|
| 2-4 | Quaint |
| 5-7 | Notable |
| 8-10 | Large |
| Jack | Strange |
| Queen | Beautiful |
| King | Dangerous |
| Ace | Secret |

Examples:
- 3♥ A small sunny meadow.
- 9♠ A large rainy creek.
- A♣ A secret hidden thicket.

## Playing the Game

Players take turns playing a card from their hand, using it to dictate the direction of the conversation, describing what their character does, says, or notices as they explore.

When all cards have been played the round is over. Update your Truths, deal new hands of 3, then reveal the next location card. You've walked on to somewhere new. Play another round of hands.

The player whose suit matches the location starts, then play continues clockwise.

After all three locations, the kids find their way back to camp.

---

### Creating the Fiction

On your turn, play a card and describe what your character does, says, or feels based on it.

Any player may respond in the moment without playing a card, but only the active player advances the story.
- If you respond, keep it short, one or two sentences then focus returns to the active player.
- If a card involves your suit, you may always respond first.

Your played card uses its colour, value, and suit together to determine where the story goes.

> When you play a card, interpret colour, value, and suit together in whatever order best fits the moment.
>
> Remember, even the smallest card can describe something incredible through the lens of a kid.

#### Changing Your Truth

At the end of each location, check if anyone played your suit. If yes, adjust your Truth by adding, removing or changing exactly one word based on what happened at this location. Write the new version underneath your old Truth.

#### Card Colour

**Red**
- About you
- You answer
- You reveal something about yourself
- Something happens to you

**Black**
- About someone else
- Ask another player
- They answer
- Something is about to happen to them

#### Card Value

**2–3: Small moment**
> > *"I found a weird rock." "My boot got muddy."*
- A small observation, a passing comment, something ordinary.

**4–7: Meaningful moment**
> *"I told her about the thing I heard dad say." "We stopped and watched it for a while."*
- Something with a bit of weight. A confidence, a feeling or a moment that lingers.

**8–10: Big moment**
> *"I slipped and nearly went into the water." "I said something I probably shouldn't have."*
- Something that changes the mood or shifts how the kids see each other.

**Jack: Imagine**
> *"What if?"*
- Pose a hypothetical. Spin out something that hasn't happened yet and let the other players respond.

**Queen: Remember**
> *"What happened before?"*
- Pull in something from earlier; before the game started, before the walk, before today.

**King: Adventure**
> *"What happens now?"*
- Something happens, the story moves. Place the King beside the current location when played.
> Kings always escalate or advance the scene before play continues.

**Ace = Reveal**
> *"What's true?"*
- Say something true. Your ace: confess something about yourself. Another ace: say something true about or directly to another player.

> At the end of the game, the Kings on the table form a trail of the afternoon's most memorable moments, a little timeline of what happened out in the woods.

#### Card Suit

The suit tells you who the card involves.

> If the suit matches your character, the card is about you. Otherwise, it is about your relationship with that character.

- **Heart** = elf
- **Diamond** = dwarf
- **Spade** = goblin
- **Club** = kobold

## Ending the Game

Once the group has returned to camp, the table answers 3 questions together:
- What did I learn about my friends?
- Who was the most surprising, and why?
- How am I different than when we started?
  - Read your first Truth, then read your last Truth.

Afterwards everyone curls up in their bedrolls and tucks away for the night.
