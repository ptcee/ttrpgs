Throwing Stones

Roll CMP + IMP. Assign one die as FUSE, one as BLAST.

FUSE = how it happens. BLAST = what it does.
Standard Action
Die Role Effect
FUSE Execution Lower = cleaner, faster, quieter
BLAST Impact Checked vs Temper. 4-6 raises Heat +1
Both 1s Smother - Reduce Heat by 3
Explosive Action [E]
Die Role Effect

BLAST # Dice Number of Explosion Dice (ExD) to roll

Explosion Die - 4+ = hit, 6 = explode again, all become GM Aftermath
Stack Up (squad combines turns)

Each dwarf throws stones. Pool all dice, assign one FUSE + one BLAST. Two or more 6s across pool = Heat +1 per participating dwarf. Cannot trigger Smother.

Group Roll (GM calls for)

Same as Stack Up rules, but treated as one throw for all outcomes. Can trigger Smother.

Squad Aftermath Dice

Before rolling, any dwarf may grab 1 Squad Aftermath die and add it to their pool. Assign FUSE/BLAST normally, discard unused die from pool.

Temper Notation

T3 = BLAST 3+ to affect, 1 ExD hit destroys.  T3[++] = BLAST 3+, needs 2 ExD hits.  T[++] = Explosives only, 2 ExD hits to destroy. Dwarves are base T3.

Simultaneous Attacks

Lower FUSE goes first. Tie: higher BLAST goes first. Still tie: d6 (1-3 enemy, 4-6 player).

Damage & Staying Alive

Hits land on gear or skills first (player's choice, fiction must support it). GRIT is last resort.

  • All gear and skills have 3 marks. Full = unavailable for mission.
  • No gear left: roll d3 for both CMP and IMP.
  • Skills can still be used until all 3 marks fill. Filled skill marks don't clear until Slag Off downtime.
  • All dwarves have 3 GRIT.

Going Down

Dwarf marks final GRIT. They take one last action. May reroll throw stones once. Then they're gone. A player or foe may choose to knock unconscious instead of kill.

Falling: ExD1 per 10ft fallen.
