Downtime Loop

Run in order after every mission. Each dwarf may take up to 2 Overhold Actions in Step 3. No dwarf may take the same action twice per downtime.

1
Debrief

Review the mission with the Hand. Did Heat hit Hammer thresholds? Primary/secondary objectives complete? Was ANVIL exposed? What rumors spread? GM narrates consequences. Distribute Ember payout into the squad vault. Adjust Squad Reputation by extraction Heat (see Rep track).

2
Recovery

Resolve Scars, funerals, retirements. All GRIT fully restored. Roll Scars for each surviving dwarf. Dwarves who died or retired roll new operatives here. Give this space - name the dead, hold the silence.

3
Overhold Actions

Each dwarf takes up to 2 actions. Burn Embers from the squad vault. No repeats. See action list at right.

4
Prep & Loadout

Review mission briefing. Each dwarf has 4 item slots. All operatives carry Runeaxe (CMP d6) and Explosives Kit (IMP d6) - these don't use slots. Requisition additional gear from the stash. Spend Embers as needed. Agree on drop point and extraction route. When loadouts are set, the mission begins.

Scars

After every mission, each surviving dwarf rolls for Scars. Roll a d6, +1d6 per GRIT marked during the mission. If any die shows 1 or 2, gain 1 Scar (max 1 per mission).

Overhold Actions

Base costs shown (at Rep 3 - Known). Rep modifies all costs. Minimum always 1 Ember.

Slag Off - 1 Ember

Drink deep, talk loud, decompress. Clear all marks on one skill. May generate a rumor - ask the GM one question during briefing.

Scout - 1-3 Ember

Reveal mission intel. 1 Ember per piece of hidden intel revealed: layout, enemy Temper values, Hammer thresholds, faction presence, or extraction complications.

Bond - 1 Ember

Create or advance a squad link. Describe how you bond. Fill 1 mark on your Bond track.

Interrogate - 1 Ember

Extract intel from prisoners or artifacts. Work on a captured enemy, document, or relic. Generates actionable intel or faction lore.

Reinforce - 2 Ember

Add temporary mission assets. Requisition a support contact, safe extract point, distraction, or borrowed heavy equipment. Single use.

Fortify - 2 Ember

Fortify a skill. Choose a Fortification, adjust the skill's Creed slightly, mark it. Max 3 fortifications per skill, no repeat types.

Forge - 3 Ember

Upgrade Runeaxe or Explosives Kit. Runeaxe gets one Blade upgrade + one Rune upgrade. Explosives Kit gets one Mix + one Casing upgrade. Roll d6 or choose with GM.

Pull Strings - 3 Ember

Manipulate Glimmerhold politics. Adjust Squad Rep by 1 mark. May affect future mission assignments.

Memorialize - 0 Ember

Honor the fallen. Add an anvil to the memorial chamber for a dead or retired operative. Adjust Squad Rep by 1 mark (up or down). May unlock a one-time benefit tied to what that dwarf was known for.
